# Policy Renewals & Retention

Ongoing relationship management after a client is bound through [[Lead Intake & Quoting]]. This is where lifetime value is made or lost.

## Cadence
- Reach out ~45 days before renewal
- Review coverage: has anything changed (new car, home improvements, new baby)?
- Check for cross-sell opportunities across [[../Services/Auto Insurance|Auto]], [[../Services/Home Insurance|Home]], [[../Services/Life Insurance|Life]]
- Confirm no lapses; explain any premium changes clearly

## Retention levers
- Multi-policy bundling reduces churn — reinforce at every renewal
- Fast, personal claims support (see client trust factors) keeps clients from shopping around

## Reporting
Renewal and retention rate feed directly into [[../Goals & KPIs]].
