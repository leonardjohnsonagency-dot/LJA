# Lead Intake & Quoting

The first touch with a prospective client, usually sourced via [[../Processes/Marketing & Content|Marketing & Content]] (website form, referral, or social).

## Steps
1. Capture basic info (name, contact, what they're shopping for — [[../Services/Auto Insurance|Auto]], [[../Services/Home Insurance|Home]], or [[../Services/Life Insurance|Life]])
2. Ask qualifying questions specific to that line
3. Run quotes across carriers
4. Present options in plain English, always mention bundling ([[../Services/Auto Insurance]] + [[../Services/Home Insurance]])
5. Close or schedule a follow-up

## Handoff
Once bound, the client moves into the [[Policy Renewals & Retention]] cycle for the life of the policy.
