# Client Segments

Who actually walks through [[Processes/Lead Intake & Quoting|the door]].

## Segments
- **Young drivers / first policy** — enter through [[Services/Auto Insurance]], price-sensitive, high shopping-around risk
- **New homeowners** — enter through [[Services/Home Insurance]], good bundling candidates for [[Services/Auto Insurance]]
- **Growing families** — best fit for [[Services/Life Insurance]], usually introduced at [[Processes/Policy Renewals & Retention|renewal]]

## Why this matters
Segment mix should shape [[Processes/Marketing & Content]] spend and messaging, and is tracked in [[Goals & KPIs]].
