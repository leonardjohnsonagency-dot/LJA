# Selling The Second Brain

This vault is also a live demo asset — the same brain-map tool that organizes [[Business Overview|our own business]] can be white-labeled and sold to other local agencies or small businesses.

## Pitch
"Here's your entire business — services, processes, goals — as an interactive galaxy you can explore and hand to new hires." Point at real examples: [[Processes/Lead Intake & Quoting]], [[Processes/Policy Renewals & Retention]], [[Goals & KPIs]].

## Delivery
White-label with the client's name/logo via `config.json` next to the skill, then re-run `/brain-map` on their notes folder.

## Ideal buyers
Any solo or small-team local business with real processes worth documenting — see [[Client Segments]] for how we think about our own buyers, as a model for theirs.
