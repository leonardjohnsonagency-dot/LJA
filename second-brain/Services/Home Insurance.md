# Home Insurance

Second most common product. Frequently bundled with [[Auto Insurance]] for the multi-policy discount.

## Coverage types offered
- Dwelling & other structures
- Personal property
- Liability & medical payments
- Loss of use

## Notes
- Homeowners often buy [[Life Insurance]] around the same life stage (new mortgage) — worth asking during [[../Processes/Lead Intake & Quoting|intake]]
- Renewal is a natural check-in point to review coverage limits after home improvements — see [[../Processes/Policy Renewals & Retention]]

## Common client segment
New homeowners and growing families — see [[../Client Segments]].
