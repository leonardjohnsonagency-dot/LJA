# Life Insurance

The hardest sell but the highest-trust product. Best introduced after a client already has [[Auto Insurance]] or [[Home Insurance]] with the agency.

## Coverage types offered
- Term life (most common starting point)
- Whole/permanent life for estate planning conversations

## Notes
- Rarely the first policy a client buys — track it as a follow-up task during [[../Processes/Policy Renewals & Retention|renewals]]
- [[../Processes/Marketing & Content|Marketing]] content about "what happens to your family if..." performs well as a soft entry point

## Common client segment
Parents, new homeowners, and anyone who just asked about [[Home Insurance]] — see [[../Client Segments]].
