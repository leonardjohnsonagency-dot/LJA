# Auto Insurance

Core product line. Most inquiries start here — often the first policy a new client buys before adding [[Home Insurance]] or [[Life Insurance]].

## Coverage types offered
- Liability (state minimums and above)
- Collision & comprehensive
- Uninsured/underinsured motorist
- Rental & roadside

## Cross-sell opportunities
- Bundle with [[Home Insurance]] for a multi-policy discount — always mention this during [[../Processes/Lead Intake & Quoting|quoting]]
- Ask about [[Life Insurance]] once trust is established, usually at [[../Processes/Policy Renewals & Retention|renewal]]

## Common client segment
Young drivers and families — see [[../Client Segments]].
